YOUR TEACHER, ON YOUR PHONE

A little Italian,
wherever you sit.

Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as your computer. Your teacher, voices and learning history stay on the computer. Keep it awake while you learn.

1. Trust this computer once

Your phone needs a trusted HTTPS connection to use its microphone. Download this computer’s Gem Teacher certificate and install it using the steps below.

Download local certificate
iPhone / iPad

Open this page in Safari. Download the certificate, then open Settings → Profile Downloaded (or General → VPN & Device Management) and install Gem Teacher Local CA. Next go to General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and enable full trust for it.

Apple’s certificate instructions ↗
Android

Download the certificate. In Settings, search for Install a certificate. Under Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials, choose Install a certificate → CA certificate, then select the downloaded file. Names vary by phone. Reopen Chrome afterward.

Google’s certificate instructions ↗
This trusts the local certificate authority created by your computer. Its private key stays on the computer. You can remove the certificate from your phone’s settings later.

2. Open your teacher

After installing the certificate, open the secure address below. Choose your learner profile, tap the microphone and allow microphone access.

Finding your local teacher…
Bookmark the secure address for next time. If your computer’s Wi-Fi address changes, restart Gem Teacher and check Preferences for the new address; the trusted certificate authority stays the same.

3. Take turns, keep the conversation

Phone and computer share the same local model. One teacher response runs at a time. Adding your phone does not load another model or reserve more GPU memory.

The profiles are available to devices on this Wi-Fi. Use your own profile to keep your progress separate.

You can type and listen over the ordinary local address too. Live microphone conversations use the secure address.
